02 — Researcher's lens
!

Retrieval helps. It doesn’t guarantee truth.

A useful RAG system depends on source quality, chunk boundaries, retrieval recall, context selection, and evaluation. A fluent answer can still omit evidence, misuse it, or cite a source that does not support the claim.

Measure retrieval and answer quality separately. Check citations against the actual retrieved passage, and provide a clear “not found” path when evidence is weak.

DESIGN PRINCIPLE · Show provenance, preserve metadata, test failure cases, and keep humans in the loop where the stakes require it.
